About Cornfield Commando

Produced in the Midwest. Proven at the range.

Firearms education, hands-on testing, and straightforward reviews from America’s heartland.

Cornfield Commando creates practical firearms content for gun owners, sport shooters, and outdoor enthusiasts. The work includes short-form educational videos, in-depth firearm and gear reviews, hands-on range testing, shooting demonstrations, and sponsor-focused product campaigns.

The channel emphasizes responsible gun ownership, safe firearm handling, realistic product expectations, and honest opinions for shooters who prefer useful information over hype.

Support the fight

Become a Gun Owners of America member.

GOA combines grassroots advocacy, legislative work, and litigation to challenge firearm restrictions and defend Second Amendment rights.

Recent efforts include backing the push that reduced NFA taxes on suppressors and short-barreled firearms to $0, challenging ATF's pistol-brace rule, and joining Silencer Shop and other plaintiffs in the 2026 case that blocked key NFA restrictions against covered plaintiffs, members, and customers.

  • $0 NFA taxes on suppressors and short-barreled firearms effective in 2026.
  • Court challenges to ATF's pistol-brace rule and its enforcement.
  • Silencer Shop v. Bondi, where a federal court permanently blocked key NFA restrictions as applied to covered plaintiffs, members, and customers.

Legal outcomes and coverage can be fact-specific. This summary is informational and not legal advice.
